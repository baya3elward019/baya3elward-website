> **Example project.** Replace the text, screenshots and links from Pages CMS.

Masrofy helps you log daily expenses, group them by category and see where the money goes each month. The whole UI is Arabic and right-to-left.

## Features

- Add, edit and delete expenses
- Categories and monthly totals
- Fully RTL layout

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
