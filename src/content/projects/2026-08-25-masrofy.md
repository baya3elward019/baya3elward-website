Masrofy helps you log daily expenses, set budgets, group spending by category and see where the money goes. The whole UI is Arabic and right-to-left, with amounts in Libyan dinar.

Everything is stored in your own browser — there is no backend and no account.

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
